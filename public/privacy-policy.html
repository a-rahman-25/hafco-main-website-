In this policy, “HAFCO”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the legal entity named above, which is the developer and data controller for the Hafco Driver Portal app and this website.

1. Information we collect

  • Hafco Driver Portal app: the app is used by HAFCO drivers and staff for dispatch and trip information. It does not require creating a public account, and it does not collect contacts, photos, precise background location, or advertising identifiers.
  • Website contact and quotation forms: name, email address, phone number and the message you choose to send us.
  • Anti-abuse verification: our forms are protected by hCaptcha, which may process limited technical data (such as IP address and browser information) to confirm submissions come from humans.
  • Local preference: your language choice (English or Arabic) is stored in your browser’s local storage.

2. How we use information

  • To answer inquiries and prepare transport quotations.
  • To contact you by phone or email about your request.
  • To operate dispatch and trip assignment for HAFCO drivers.
  • To protect our services from spam and abuse.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Third-party services

  • Secure cloud hosting, database and email delivery for form submissions.
  • hCaptcha — bot protection; hCaptcha’s own privacy policy applies to its processing.
  • Google Fonts — typography delivery on the website.
  • Google Play — app distribution and crash/health reporting provided by Google.

4. Data sharing, storage and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on secured cloud servers with access restricted to authorised HAFCO staff. We disclose data only where required by law, to protect our legal rights, or to the service providers listed above strictly so they can operate the service for us.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep inquiry correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request and for our business records. To access, correct or delete personal data you have provided — including app account data for drivers — email info@hafcobigmover.com with your request and we will action it within a reasonable period, normally 30 days.

6. Children’s privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version is always published at this URL.
